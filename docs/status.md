# Initiative Status

!!! warning "Health-RI Semantic Interoperability Initiative: discontinued"

    The Health-RI Semantic Interoperability Initiative was formally paused on **May 1, 2026** and has since been **discontinued**.

    No further development, curation, community review, or release activity is planned under this initiative.

## Archival status

The documentation site, repository history, released semantic artifacts, publications, and persistent identifiers remain available as archival resources for reference, reuse, and citation.

These resources are **not actively maintained**. In particular:

- no new HRIO, HRIV, SSSOM mapping-set, or project-documentation releases are planned;
- the documented governance, validation, contribution, and publication procedures are retained as records of the processes used during the initiative and are no longer operational;
- contribution and community-review workflows are closed;
- known limitations in released artifacts may remain unresolved.

## Persistent identifiers and citations

Existing w3id persistent identifiers remain the stable access points for published resources. Unversioned or `latest` identifiers now resolve to the **final available published artifacts** and are not expected to advance under this initiative.

For reproducible citations, continue to prefer version-specific identifiers and release-specific DOIs where available.

## Historical timeline

- **May 1, 2026** — the initiative was formally paused.
- **August 18, 2026** — the public project documentation was updated to record that the initiative had been discontinued and to place the repository and documentation in archival status.
